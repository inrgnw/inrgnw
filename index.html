INRGNW

Energy expertise with a Pacific Northwest perspective.

inrgNW is a Washington-based sole proprietorship focused on helping building owners and organizations understand energy use, meet regulatory requirements, plan capital investments, and identify practical opportunities for improvement.

WHAT WE DO

Focused services. Practical results.

CB

Clean Buildings Act

Washington State Clean Buildings Performance Standard support, including Tier 1 compliance, documentation, energy management planning, and exemptions.

SE

Strategic Energy Management

Turn energy data into an actionable management process that identifies savings opportunities and supports lasting operational improvements.

CP

Capital Planning

Connect building condition, energy performance, equipment needs, and long-term capital planning to make better investment decisions.

EA

Equipment Audits

Field-focused reviews of building systems and equipment to identify operational issues, replacement priorities, and efficiency opportunities.

ES

ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager

Benchmarking, tracking, data organization, and reporting support using ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager.
